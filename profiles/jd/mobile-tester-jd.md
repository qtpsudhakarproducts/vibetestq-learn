# Mobile Tester

## Job Title
Mobile QA Engineer

## Location
Lucknow, India

## Experience Required
2-4 years

## Key Skills
Appium, Android/iOS Testing, Mobile Automation, Device Farms, Functional Testing

## Job Description
Join our mobile team as a QA Engineer to ensure the quality of our mobile applications across Android and iOS platforms. This role focuses on functional testing and automation.

## Responsibilities
- Design and execute test cases for mobile applications
- Perform functional, usability, and compatibility testing
- Develop automated test scripts using Appium
- Test on various devices and OS versions
- Manage device farms and cloud testing services
- Log and track mobile-specific defects
- Collaborate with mobile developers and designers
- Ensure app store compliance and guidelines

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- 2+ years of experience in mobile testing
- Proficiency in Appium or similar automation tools
- Knowledge of Android and iOS platforms
- Understanding of mobile UI/UX principles
- Experience with device farms is a plus

## Benefits
- Competitive salary and incentives
- Mobile device allowances
- Flexible work options
- Learning and development support
