# Accessibility Tester

## Job Title
Accessibility Tester

## Location
Vadodara, India

## Experience Required
1-3 years

## Key Skills
WCAG, Axe-core, Screen Readers, Accessibility Audits, Assistive Technologies

## Job Description
As an Accessibility Tester, you will ensure our digital products are accessible to users with disabilities. This role involves conducting accessibility audits and providing remediation guidance.

## Responsibilities
- Perform accessibility audits using WCAG guidelines
- Test with screen readers and assistive technologies
- Identify accessibility violations and issues
- Provide detailed reports and remediation recommendations
- Collaborate with designers and developers on accessible design
- Integrate accessibility checks into CI pipelines
- Stay updated with accessibility standards and best practices
- Educate teams on accessibility importance

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, HCI, or related field
- 1+ years of experience in accessibility testing
- Knowledge of WCAG 2.1 and accessibility principles
- Familiarity with testing tools like Axe-core
- Understanding of assistive technologies
- Strong attention to detail and empathy for user needs

## Benefits
- Competitive salary
- Accessibility training opportunities
- Inclusive workplace culture
- Flexible working hours
