# Senior Test Lead

## Job Title
Senior Test Lead

## Location
Lucknow, India

## Experience Required
8-10 years

## Key Skills/Technologies
Test Leadership, Team Management, QA Strategy, Process Improvement, Stakeholder Management, Risk Assessment

## Job Description
Lead testing teams and projects as a Senior Test Lead. This role focuses on team management, quality assurance, and delivery excellence.

## Responsibilities
- Lead testing teams for multiple projects
- Develop and implement test strategies
- Manage team performance and development
- Coordinate with project stakeholders
- Assess and mitigate testing risks
- Drive quality improvements
- Report on testing status and metrics
- Ensure compliance with standards

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in relevant field
- 8-10 years of testing leadership experience
- Strong management and leadership skills
- Experience in complex project environments
- Risk management knowledge
- Excellent communication abilities

## Benefits
- Senior management salary
- Leadership bonuses
- Team development budget
- Work-life balance
