# Lead Test Lead

## Job Title
Lead Test Lead

## Location
Ahmedabad, India

## Experience Required
9-12 years

## Key Skills/Technologies
Test Management, Team Leadership, QA Strategy, Automation Oversight, Stakeholder Management, Process Improvement

## Job Description
As a Lead Test Lead, you will oversee multiple testing teams and projects. This senior leadership role focuses on strategic QA direction and team development.

## Responsibilities
- Lead multiple QA teams and projects
- Develop and implement QA strategies
- Manage testing budgets and resources
- Coordinate with senior stakeholders
- Drive process improvements and innovations
- Mentor test leads and managers
- Ensure compliance with quality standards
- Report on quality metrics to executive team

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's degree
- 9-12 years of QA leadership experience
- Proven team management skills
- Experience in large-scale projects
- Strong strategic thinking
- Excellent communication skills
