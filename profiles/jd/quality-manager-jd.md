# Quality Manager

## Job Title
Quality Manager

## Location
Delhi, India

## Experience Required
10-15 years

## Key Skills
Quality Governance, Metrics, Compliance, Process Improvements, Team Leadership

## Job Description
Lead quality assurance across the organization as a Quality Manager. This senior role involves setting QA standards, driving improvements, and ensuring compliance with quality objectives.

## Responsibilities
- Define and implement QA policies and standards
- Establish quality metrics and KPIs
- Conduct quality audits and assessments
- Lead process improvement initiatives
- Manage QA teams and resources
- Collaborate with senior leadership on quality strategy
- Ensure compliance with industry standards
- Drive a culture of quality across the organization

## Qualifications
- Master's degree in Computer Science or Business Administration
- 10+ years of experience in QA with management roles
- Strong knowledge of quality management systems
- Experience in process improvement methodologies
- Excellent leadership and communication skills
- Certifications like CSQA or PMP are advantageous

## Benefits
- Executive-level compensation
- Leadership development programs
- Comprehensive benefits package
- Strategic decision-making opportunities
