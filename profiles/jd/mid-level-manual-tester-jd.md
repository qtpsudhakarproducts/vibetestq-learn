# Mid-level Manual Tester

## Job Title
Mid-level Manual Tester

## Location
Mumbai, India

## Experience Required
3-5 years

## Key Skills/Technologies
Manual Testing, Test Planning, JIRA, TestRail, SQL, Exploratory Testing, Agile, Regression Testing, UAT

## Job Description
We are looking for an experienced Mid-level Manual Tester to lead testing efforts for complex applications. This role involves test planning, execution, and team collaboration.

## Responsibilities
- Create comprehensive test plans and strategies
- Design and execute detailed test cases
- Perform functional, integration, and system testing
- Coordinate UAT with business stakeholders
- Analyze test results and provide quality reports
- Mentor junior testers and review their work
- Identify process improvements and implement them
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- 3-5 years of manual testing experience
- Proficiency in test management tools
- Strong knowledge of testing methodologies
- Experience in Agile environments
- Excellent communication and documentation skills
