# Senior QA Engineer

## Job Title
Senior QA Engineer

## Location
Chennai, India

## Experience Required
6-8 years

## Key Skills/Technologies
Test Strategy, Automation Frameworks, Selenium/Playwright, Performance Testing, Security Testing, Leadership, Mentoring

## Job Description
Lead quality assurance initiatives as a Senior QA Engineer. This role involves strategic testing, team guidance, and ensuring product excellence across complex systems.

## Responsibilities
- Develop and implement QA strategies
- Lead test planning and execution for major releases
- Design and maintain automation frameworks
- Perform advanced testing types (performance, security)
- Mentor junior QA engineers
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams
- Analyze quality metrics and drive improvements
- Represent QA in cross-functional discussions

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science
- 6-8 years of QA experience
- Expertise in multiple testing domains
- Leadership and mentoring experience
- Strong technical and analytical skills
- Experience with Agile and DevOps

## Benefits
- Senior-level compensation
- Leadership development
- Comprehensive benefits
- Work-life balance
