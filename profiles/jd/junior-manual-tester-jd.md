# Junior Manual Tester

## Job Title
Junior Manual Tester

## Location
Bangalore, India

## Experience Required
0-2 years

## Key Skills
Manual Testing, Test Case Design, Defect Tracking, JIRA, SQL, Agile, Regression Testing

## Job Description
We are looking for a detail-oriented Junior Manual Tester to join our QA team. This role involves executing manual test cases, identifying defects, and ensuring software quality in web and mobile applications.

## Responsibilities
- Design and execute manual test cases for functional and regression testing
- Log and track defects using JIRA or similar tools
- Perform smoke and sanity testing
- Collaborate with developers and product teams in Agile environments
- Document test results and prepare reports
- Participate in sprint planning and reviews
- Learn automation basics and contribute to test automation efforts

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- Basic understanding of software testing principles
- Familiarity with bug tracking tools like JIRA
- Good analytical and communication skills
- Knowledge of SQL for database testing is a plus

## Benefits
- Competitive salary
- Health insurance
- Learning and development opportunities
- Flexible work hours
