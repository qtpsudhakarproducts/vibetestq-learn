# Senior Automation Engineer

## Job Title
Senior Automation Engineer

## Location
Delhi, India

## Experience Required
6-8 years

## Key Skills/Technologies
Playwright, Cypress, Selenium, TypeScript/JavaScript, CI/CD, Docker, Kubernetes, Test Architecture

## Job Description
As a Senior Automation Engineer, you will architect and lead automation efforts for large-scale applications. This role focuses on scalable test solutions and team leadership.

## Responsibilities
- Architect scalable automation frameworks
- Lead automation strategy and implementation
- Develop advanced test automation solutions
- Integrate with containerized and cloud environments
- Optimize CI/CD pipelines for testing
- Mentor automation team members
- Collaborate on test infrastructure design
- Drive innovation in testing technologies

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science
- 6-8 years of automation engineering experience
- Expertise in modern automation tools
- Experience with cloud and container technologies
- Strong leadership and architectural skills
- Knowledge of DevOps practices

## Benefits
- High compensation package
- Technology innovation budget
- Leadership opportunities
- Flexible work arrangements
