# Senior Manual Tester

## Job Title
Senior Manual Tester

## Location
Jaipur, India

## Experience Required
6-8 years

## Key Skills/Technologies
Manual Testing, Test Strategy, Domain Expertise, UAT, Defect Management, Mentoring, Process Optimization

## Job Description
As a Senior Manual Tester, you will lead manual testing efforts for complex applications. This role involves deep domain knowledge and team guidance.

## Responsibilities
- Lead manual testing for critical projects
- Develop test strategies and plans
- Coordinate UAT and acceptance testing
- Provide domain-specific testing expertise
- Mentor junior manual testers
- Optimize testing processes
- Collaborate with business analysts
- Ensure thorough defect coverage

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in relevant field
- 6-8 years of manual testing experience
- Domain knowledge in specific industry
- Leadership and mentoring skills
- Strong analytical abilities
- Experience in complex testing scenarios

## Benefits
- Competitive salary
- Domain expertise recognition
- Career advancement
- Work stability
