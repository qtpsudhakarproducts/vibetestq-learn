# Lead QA Engineer

## Location

## Experience Required
9-12 years

## Key Skills/Technologies
QA Leadership, Test Automation, Quality Strategy, Team Management, Process Excellence, Technology Evaluation

Lead QA engineering as a Lead QA Engineer, driving quality across the organization. This role combines technical expertise with leadership responsibilities.

- Lead QA engineering initiatives
- Develop quality strategies and roadmaps
- Manage QA teams and projects
- Evaluate and implement QA technologies

- Collaborate with engineering leadership
- Ensure quality in product development
- Mentor QA professionals

- 9-12 years of QA experience
- Strong technical and leadership skills
- Experience in QA transformation
- Knowledge of modern testing practices
- Strategic thinking abilities
