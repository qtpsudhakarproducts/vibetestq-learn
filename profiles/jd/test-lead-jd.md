# Test Lead

## Job Title
Test Lead

## Location
Ahmedabad, India

## Experience Required
7-10 years

## Key Skills
Test Strategy, Team Management, Automation Planning, Stakeholder Communication, QA Processes

## Job Description
Lead our QA team as a Test Lead, overseeing testing activities across multiple projects. This role involves defining test strategies, managing resources, and ensuring quality delivery.

## Responsibilities
- Define and implement QA strategies and processes
- Lead and mentor a team of QA engineers
- Plan testing efforts and allocate resources
- Coordinate with stakeholders and manage expectations
- Oversee test execution and defect management
- Ensure compliance with quality standards
- Drive continuous improvement in testing practices
- Report on quality metrics and project status

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or related field
- 7+ years of experience in QA with leadership roles
- Strong knowledge of testing methodologies and tools
- Experience in Agile and Waterfall environments
- Excellent communication and leadership skills
- Certification in QA or project management is a plus

## Benefits
- Competitive salary and bonuses
- Leadership development programs
- Health and retirement benefits
- Flexible work arrangements
