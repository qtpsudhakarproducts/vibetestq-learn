# Junior QA Engineer

## Job Title
Junior QA Engineer

## Location
Bangalore, India

## Experience Required
0-2 years

## Key Skills/Technologies
Manual Testing, Basic Automation, JIRA, Test Case Design, SQL, Agile, Regression Testing, Bug Tracking

## Job Description
We are seeking a Junior QA Engineer to join our quality assurance team. This entry-level role focuses on learning testing fundamentals, executing test cases, and contributing to software quality.

## Responsibilities
- Execute manual test cases and document results
- Assist in creating and maintaining test cases
- Log defects and track them using JIRA
- Perform basic regression and smoke testing
- Collaborate with development team in Agile sprints
- Learn automation tools and scripting basics
- Participate in daily stand-ups and sprint reviews
- Support test environment setup and maintenance

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- Fresh graduate or 0-2 years of experience in testing
- Basic understanding of software development lifecycle
- Good analytical and communication skills
- Willingness to learn new technologies
- Knowledge of databases and SQL is a plus

## Benefits
- Competitive starting salary
- Health insurance
- Learning and development programs
- Flexible work hours
- Career growth opportunities
