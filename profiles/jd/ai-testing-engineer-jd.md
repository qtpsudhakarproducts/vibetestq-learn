# AI Testing Engineer

## Job Title
AI Testing Engineer

## Location
Hyderabad, India

## Experience Required
2-4 years

## Key Skills
AI/ML Testing, Model Validation, Python, Pytest, Data Testing, Bias Detection, Basic ML Knowledge

## Job Description
Join our team as an AI Testing Engineer to validate AI/ML models and ensure their accuracy, robustness, and fairness. This role involves designing tests for model evaluation and collaborating with data scientists.

## Responsibilities
- Design and execute tests for AI/ML model accuracy and performance
- Validate training and test datasets for quality and bias
- Build evaluation pipelines and monitoring systems
- Perform robustness testing and edge case analysis
- Collaborate with ML engineers to reproduce and fix issues
- Document test results and provide recommendations
- Stay updated with AI testing best practices

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related field
- Experience in software testing with focus on AI/ML
- Proficiency in Python and testing frameworks like Pytest
- Understanding of ML concepts and evaluation metrics
- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail

## Benefits
- Competitive salary package
- Learning opportunities in AI/ML
- Flexible working environment
- Career growth prospects
