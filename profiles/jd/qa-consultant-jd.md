# QA Consultant

## Job Title
QA Consultant

## Location
Pune, India

## Experience Required
12-18 years

## Key Skills
QA Transformation, Process Audits, Automation Roadmap, Strategy, Mentoring

## Job Description
As a QA Consultant, you will help organizations transform their QA practices, adopt automation, and improve delivery quality. This senior role involves strategic consulting and implementation.

## Responsibilities
- Assess current QA maturity and identify improvement areas
- Design automation roadmaps and tool selections
- Conduct QA process audits and provide recommendations
- Lead QA transformation initiatives
- Mentor teams and build internal capabilities
- Develop training programs for QA best practices
- Collaborate with leadership on quality strategy
- Measure and report on transformation outcomes

## Qualifications
- Master's degree in Computer Science or related field
- 12+ years of experience in QA with consulting background
- Deep knowledge of QA methodologies and automation
- Experience in organizational change management
- Strong presentation and influencing skills
- Certifications in QA leadership or consulting

## Benefits
- High-level compensation and bonuses
- Consulting project incentives
- Travel opportunities
- Executive networking
