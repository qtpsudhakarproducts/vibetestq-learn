## Professional summary

- Quality Engineering practitioner focused on permission-boundary and negative api validation.

## Responsibilities

- Owns complex workflows or a technical area. Reviews approaches, diagnoses difficult failures, and improves the team's validation practices.
- Agree the allowed scope before running specialist checks.
- Document reproducible failures and their impact without unsupported severity claims.
- Work with engineering to verify remediation and regression coverage.

- Reproducible defect evidence
- Test scoping and written permission

- Risk communication
