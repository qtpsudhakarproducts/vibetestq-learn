The Mission

What Is the Mentor Role?

As a VibeTestQ Mentor, you will power our Free Interview Service by supporting professionals through simulated technical discussions focused on engineering thinking and decision-making.

Before starting, you'll work with the founder to:

  • Guide professionals beyond technical checklists
  • Cultivate engineering mindset & system thinking
  • Analyze risk and quality strategy beyond tools
  • Ensure fairness, empathy, and professionalism
  • Provide growth-oriented, actionable feedback
Exclusive Access

Founder-Led Guidance

QtpSudhakar, Founder of VibeTestQ, connects with mentors every week for alignment and learning.

  • Weekly Connects: Direct access to the founder for guidance
  • Mentor Community: Dedicated group for peer interaction
  • Alignment: Ongoing discussions on mentorship impact
  • Networking: Exclusive invitations to community meetups and parties
The Rewards

What Mentors Gain

This is a non-monetary role where the value lies in learning and impact.

  • Improved mentorship and leadership skills
  • Increased professional visibility in the community
  • Trusted peer network and meaningful connections
  • Credential and credibility as a VibeTestQ Mentor
The Commitment

What's Expected

  • Conduct discussions professionally and respectfully
  • Document observations and constructive feedback
  • Attend weekly connects with the founder
  • Stay aware of Industry and Quality Engineering trends
  • Demonstrate a commitment to serve others
Clarification

What This Is Not

  • Not a paid or commercial program
  • Not a hiring or recruitment role
  • Not volume-based checklist assessments
  • Not a platform for personal promotions
  • Not a short-term or transactional engagement

Mentorship Ethics & Principles

Sessions are reviewed for mentorship excellence and standardizing impact. Mentors must adhere to the highest standards of fairness, integrity, and confidentiality.

The Purpose

Why This Program Exists

Today, learning content is available everywhere. But professionals often leave technical discussions without understanding what actually went wrong. VibeTestQ bridges that gap through transparent, thoughtful, and respectful guidance — providing clarity before struggle and direction for success.

Mentors are the backbone of this mission.
