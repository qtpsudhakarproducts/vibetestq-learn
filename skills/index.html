Prompting, Context & Skills

A Complete Guide for Testers and Test Automation Engineers

"The quality of your AI output is not determined by how smart the model is. It is determined by how well you set it up to succeed."

What This Guide Teaches

This guide teaches you three things that every tester working with AI needs to know:

Prompt Engineering

How to write instructions that get useful, specific output from an AI model every single time. Not sometimes. Every time.

Context Engineering

How to control what the AI knows before it answers. This is the difference between output that is technically correct and output that is correct for your project, your team, and your standards.

Skills (SKILL.md)

How to encode your standards once and have them applied automatically across every session, every tool, and every engineer on your team. Zero setup per session. Consistent output forever.

Complete System: Each concept builds on the previous. Together they form a complete system for using AI in testing work — from writing a single test case to enforcing team-wide quality standards.

Who This Guide Is For

Manual Testers

Who have tried AI tools and gotten inconsistent results. You want to understand why and fix it permanently. You do not need to write code. Everything in your reading path is written in plain English.

Automation Engineers

Who write Playwright, Cypress, Selenium, or similar frameworks. You want AI to produce code that matches your project's conventions without re-explaining them every session.

QA Leads and Managers

Who want to standardise how their team uses AI tools and make quality standards self-enforcing rather than dependent on individuals.
