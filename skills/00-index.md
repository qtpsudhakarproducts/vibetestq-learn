## What This Book Teaches

This book teaches you three things that every tester working with AI needs to know.

**Prompt Engineering** — how to write instructions that get useful, specific output from an AI model every single time. Not sometimes. Every time.

**Context Engineering** — how to control what the AI knows before it answers. This is the difference between output that is technically correct and output that is correct for your project, your team, and your standards.

**Skills (SKILL.md)** — how to encode your standards once and have them applied automatically across every session, every tool, and every engineer on your team. Zero setup per session. Consistent output forever.

Each one builds on the previous. Together they form a complete system for using AI in testing work — from writing a single test case to enforcing team-wide quality standards.

---

## Who This Book Is For

**Manual testers** who have tried AI tools and gotten inconsistent results. You want to understand why and fix it permanently. You do not need to write code. Everything in your reading path is written in plain English.

**Automation engineers** who write Playwright, Cypress, Selenium, or similar frameworks. You want AI to produce code that matches your project's conventions without re-explaining them every session.

**QA leads and managers** who want to standardise how their team uses AI tools and make quality standards self-enforcing rather than dependent on individuals.

## The Progression Map

Here is where you will be at each level after working through this book.

**Level 1 — Better Prompts**
You write clearer instructions. Output improves for simple, one-off tasks. You understand why vague prompts produce vague output.

**Level 2 — Consistent Sessions**
You load the right context at the start of every session. Output is consistent and project-specific throughout. You stop re-explaining the same things.

**Level 3 — Permanent Standards**
You have a skill that loads your standards automatically. You no longer need a session start ritual. Output is correct from the first turn of every session.

**Level 4 — Team Infrastructure**
Your skills live in the repository. Every engineer on the team gets the same standards automatically. New team members produce correct output from Day 1.
