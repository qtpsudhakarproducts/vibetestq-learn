DAY 05

Special Contexts: Dialogs, Frames & Windows

These are not element types. They are special contexts.

Key Concepts

  • Dialogs: Set listener page.on('dialog') BEFORE trigger.
  • iFrames: Use frameLocator() to scope your actions.
  • Windows: context.waitForEvent('page') to capture new tabs.
  • Downloads: Intercept with page.waitForDownload().
